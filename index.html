The Orvante journal / Field notes

    
A little more
behind the details.

    

      
01 / Getting startedFrom a rough sketch to a clean CAD model.
A useful starting brief includes the object’s purpose, overall dimensions, reference images, and how it will be made. Clear constraints matter more than a polished sketch. We agree on the scope and deliverables before modeling begins.

      
02 / Better handoffsNative files, neutral formats, and why both matter.
Native files preserve the feature history that makes future edits easier. Neutral formats such as STEP and IGES help different CAD systems exchange geometry. A considered handoff includes the right formats, consistent units, and a clear file structure—not just a single export.

      
03 / Production thinkingA drawing package your fabricator can use.
A production drawing should answer questions before they reach the workshop. Start with critical dimensions, suitable tolerances, material and finish notes, and an unambiguous revision history. The final requirements should always be agreed with your manufacturing partner.
