The sheet a client or a patent office asks for.

Some clients do not want a pretty picture. They want one sheet that answers every question: what it looks like, how big it is, how it comes apart, and what each part is called. Both sheets below were produced from the 3D model.

What this proves

A model is only half the job. The sheet is what a fabricator, a consultant or an examiner actually reads, so the layout, the callouts and the parts list are built with the same care as the geometry. Panels get 13 fixed views; products get a figure package; civil work gets sections and elevations.

Six MCC panels, 78 sheets, one layout.

Where we stop.

Models are layout and approval grade: dimensionally faithful to the drawing, every part separate and named. We will tell you which of the following your job needs, and say no if it is not ours to do.

  • Fabrication-grade sheet-metal flat patterns
  • GD&T manufacturing drawings
  • Structural design
  • Busbar sizing and electrical calculations

Also available. Reverse modelling from a sample, scan or marked print; exploded service views for manuals; re-rendering of an existing STEP file in a new finish or colourway. None of it needs a new drawing.
